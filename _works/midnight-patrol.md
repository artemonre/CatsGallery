title: Midnight Patrol

The house is his when we're asleep:
the stairs, the hall, the kitchen tile.
He walks his rounds on velvet feet
and checks each shadow for a while.

By morning he is curled and still,
as if he'd never been awake,
but every door is left ajar —
his proof, for honesty's sake.
